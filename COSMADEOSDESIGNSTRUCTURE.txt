How This Should Translate to Cosmade OS

Every primary department should adopt this exact structural pattern rather than the current dashboard-centric layout.

For example, opening People & HR should present a left workspace navigation with grouped sections (Workforce, Recruitment, Learning, Performance, Policies, Settings) and the main content area should always be a productivity page (employee directory, handbook, recruitment pipeline, etc.), not a collection of dashboard cards.

The same applies to every department:

Sales: grouped navigation for Leads, Companies, Contacts, Meetings, Playbooks, Knowledge, Reports, with the main area showing CRM or playbooks rather than KPIs.
Marketing: grouped navigation for Campaigns, Creative Library, Content Calendar, Brand Assets, Research, Reports.
Finance: grouped navigation for Revenue, Expenses, Runway, Cash Flow, Budget Planning, Forecasting, Invoices, Bills, Banking, Reports.
Operations: grouped navigation for SOPs, Procurement, Vendors, Inventory, Compliance, Requests, Audit Logs.
Knowledge Base: organized like Notion, with sections for handbooks, SOPs, templates, meeting notes, lessons learned, and best practices.
Training Center: organized like a corporate LMS with learning paths, courses, assessments, certifications, progress, and departmental training.

The overall principle should remain consistent across the entire platform:

A global navigation switches between major company workspaces.
A workspace sidebar exposes the tools and knowledge within the selected department.
A main content area is dedicated to executing work.
Dashboards exist as overview pages, but most pages prioritize knowledge, processes, collaboration, and execution.
