# AI Interaction Standards

> Reference note — the interaction-rule counterpart to [07 Enterprise AI/global-ai-experience.md](global-ai-experience.md).

## Standard

AI should never interrupt work. AI appears as: Suggestions, Summaries, Recommendations, Insights, Drafts, Predictions. Users remain in control. Every AI-generated action is clearly labeled.

## Relationship to Other Standards

This standard is one of the platform's cross-cutting UX standards defined in Foundation Volume 1, Chapter 10, alongside Performance and Accessibility Interaction Standards — see [11 UX System/accessibility-performance-standards.md](../11%20UX%20System/accessibility-performance-standards.md).

## Enforcement

Every applicable feature is checked against this standard as part of the "Intelligence" section of the [Enterprise Quality Checklist](../11%20UX%20System/enterprise-quality-checklist.md): search indexing configured, AI integration evaluated, relationships established, activity timeline enabled.
